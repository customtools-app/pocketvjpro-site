Pocket VJ Pro Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Pocket VJ Pro is made by CustomTools.App LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Pocket VJ Pro app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

The short version

We don't collect your personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking. The app works on your device, and the few things that leave it go to Apple's services under your own Apple Account. The free version on iPhone and iPad shows non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, and Google's software collects some data to show them (see Ads, below). Pro removes the ads.

What the app uses, and why

Microphone. When you turn on MIC, the app listens so the visuals can follow the music. The sound is analyzed on your device into levels, beats and tempo. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The Beat Recorder keeps only the timing of the hits, in memory, while you record.

Sound from your Mac. On a Mac, if you choose Mac sound, the app listens to what your Mac is playing in the same way: analyzed on the device, never recorded or sent.

Camera, photos and files. Only when you pick them in Inputs. A photo, video or file you choose is shown in the studio on your device. The camera picture is used live and is not recorded unless you start a recording yourself.

Recordings and Library. Recordings you make are saved on your device, in the app's Library. If you turn on iCloud Drive for the Library, they are stored in your own iCloud Drive.

Local network. Used only when you host a studio, play a nearby studio, send pictures to another screen, or connect a TV tuner on your network. This traffic stays on your local network between your own devices.

Motion. If you turn on Gyro, the app reads your device's motion to move the picture. Nothing is stored or sent.

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, your presets, looks, shows and the list of your devices sync through your own private iCloud database, run by Apple. We cannot see this data.

Purchases. Pro and Ultra are one-time in-app purchases handled by Apple. We receive no payment details; Apple tells the app only what you own.

Ads (free version, iPhone and iPad)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob in a small bar at the bottom of your own device's screen, and, only if you tap a button to watch, short videos (for example to unlock the next item on your unlock path, or for an hour with no ads). Ads never appear on the picture your audience sees: not on external screens, AirPlay or Apple TV, recordings, streams or tile walls. Those show only our own small card with the CustomTools.App logo and a QR code to the App Store page.

  • Non-personalized only. We ask Google for non-personalized ads on every request. We do not use App Tracking Transparency and never ask to track you.
  • Teen-safe settings. Every request is marked for a teen audience and limited to ads rated for teens. Videos never play on their own.
  • What Google's software may collect to show an ad, count it and prevent fraud: your device's approximate location (from its internet address), an identifier specific to this app or Google's software, which ads were shown and tapped, and crash and performance data. Google describes this at developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure and in its policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Your choices in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The app asks for your choices first, through Google's consent form, and keeps an "Ad privacy choices" link on the Home screen.
  • No ads with Pro. With Pro the app never contacts Google's ad services. The Mac, Apple Vision Pro and Apple TV apps have no Google ads.

The CustomTools.App card. On the free version the picture carries a small card with our logo and a QR code to the App Store page. It contains no tracking, and the app collects nothing when someone scans it. With Pro, a band can hide it or show its own QR code or sponsors' logos instead; that sponsor information stays on your device and in files you export.

Crash reports. If you share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, track you across apps or websites, use third-party analytics, or build profiles. We don't collect your name, email or location ourselves. Apart from the ads described above in the free version, nothing about you goes to anyone else.

Children

Pocket VJ Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
